
# Desert Kingdom - Art Assets

## Project
Game Name: Sultans' Sands
Repository: DesertKingdom
Engine: Unity 2022.3.62f2
Platform: Android
Graphics: 3D Realistic Isometric

## Art Direction
Create a realistic and detailed Arabian desert kingdom inspired by high-quality mobile strategy games.

## Required Asset Categories

### 1. Royal Palace
- Large Arabian royal palace.
- Detailed Arabic architecture.
- Domes, arches, balconies and towers.
- Sandstone and golden decorative materials.

### 2. Defensive Structures
- Detailed defensive walls.
- Watchtowers.
- Main kingdom gates.
- Defensive buildings.

### 3. Kingdom Buildings
- Houses.
- Barracks.
- Farms.
- Workshops.
- Markets.
- Resource storage buildings.

### 4. Environment
- Realistic desert terrain.
- Winding sandy roads.
- Small green oases.
- Water features.
- No large mountains or oversized trees.

### 5. Materials and Textures
- Sandstone.
- Desert sand.
- Ancient stone.
- Wood.
- Metal.
- Water and vegetation.

## Technical Requirements
- Compatible with Unity 2022.3.62f2.
- Android optimized.
- Prefer low-poly optimized geometry with realistic textures.
- Use Level of Detail (LOD) where appropriate.
- Reuse materials and textures.
- Prefer FBX and PNG formats.
- Include licensing information for every external asset.

## Important Rules
- No placeholder cubes as final buildings.
- No manually drawn assets.
- Use ready-made or AI-generated 3D assets.
- Maintain a consistent Arabian desert kingdom style.
- Prioritize visual quality and mobile performance.
